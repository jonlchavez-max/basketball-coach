Knicks Playbook
Simple cues for 3rd grade. Short passes, help the ball, move, rebound, get back.
HELP THE BALL

When a teammate picks up the dribble, the nearest teammate comes toward the ball. Everyone else stays useful.

CALL: “HELP — DON’T CROWD!”
PASS → MOVE

After passing, move immediately. Cut toward the basket or into a useful short passing lane.

CALL: “PASS AND MOVE!”
SHOT → REBOUND

No watching. The ball goes up and we attack the boards.

CALL: “SHOT — GO!”
TURNOVER → BACK

First job is to get between the ball and our basket. Then find a player.

CALL: “BACK!”
MEET THE PASS

Receivers come toward the ball. Prefer chest and bounce passes over lobs.

CALL: “GO GET IT!”
SEE TWO

On defense, see your player and the ball. Keep hands up and stay active.

CALL: “SEE TWO — HANDS UP!”
TEAM REMINDERS
Dribble → pick up → pass or shoot • No lobs • Keep moving • Bigs crash the boards • Come toward the pass
